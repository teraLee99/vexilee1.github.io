About

Hi there!

This is my passion project all about camping, nature and nonsense. I drive my lil car to public lands, take pictures of trees, then work on a crochet project while I watch the sunset. At least, that's usually what I'm up to. The trips I go on bring me a lot of joy, and I love sharing them with others.

As for the name Vexilee, it's just nonsense. It means whatever you want it to. I take inspiration from the idea that life is nonsense; Vexilee captures that idea for me. It's not to say that life makes no sense, it's more you get to make it up as you go. For me, that often involves a mountain view and string lights in the car.

Thank you for being here!
